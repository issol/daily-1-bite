개인정보처리방침 / Privacy Policy

Airout finds piled-up screenshots, look-alikes and shaky shots on your iPhone and lets you air them out ten at a time. Everything runs on-device; photos never leave your phone. No server, no account. Aired-out photos stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days. Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. Questions: isolatorv@gmail.com
